About

Travel content sells trips. Nothing proves it.

A creator posts a cabin on a storm-lashed coast. Four thousand people save it. Eleven of them go. Nobody — not the creator, not the property, not the platform — can point to a single one of those trips and say that post did that. FETCH exists to close that gap.

The gap in the middle

Intent for a trip is created long before anyone opens a search bar. It happens in a Reel at 11pm, in a Story someone screenshots, in a two-year-old TikTok about a town nobody had heard of. By the time a traveller reaches a booking page, the decision is largely made — and the platform that takes 20% of it had nothing to do with making it.

Meanwhile the person who actually created that intent gets paid in exposure, or gets a flat fee that has no relationship to what the post did. And the independent property paying the toll never learns which content brought them a guest.

  • Creators monetise everything about travel except the travel itself
  • Properties pay the most for the guests they're least able to keep
  • Travellers book somewhere worse than what they were shown, because it was easier
  • Nobody holds the record that connects the content to the booking

What FETCH is

An attribution and commission layer for direct travel bookings. A creator tags a place in content they already made; a traveller taps through and books direct on the property's own engine; the booking is verified; the creator gets paid in cash on the completed stay.

1

Not a marketplace

We don't want travellers browsing FETCH. We want them booking you, direct, from the content that inspired them.

2

Not a booking engine

The property's engine takes the booking. FETCH doesn't compete with it and never will.

3

Not an advertising product

Nothing is charged for reach, impressions or placement. If no booking happens, nobody pays anything.

4

Invisible infrastructure

The traveller never learns FETCH exists. The best version of this product is one nobody sees.

Four rules we don't break

Never hold the money

FETCH never touches the traveller's payment and never becomes merchant of record. The property is paid directly, always.

Never become a destination

No competing booking engine, no consumer marketplace. The moment travellers search FETCH instead of you, we've broken the model.

Never charge to join

No listing fees, no subscriptions, no charge for hosted stays. Free for both sides, permanently.

Never creep the take rate

10%, split evenly. New fees would make FETCH another toll — which is the thing it was built to remove.

Where we are right now

Pilot phase, and deliberately unglamorous. The whole job at this stage is answering one question honestly.

NowProve the number

One vertical on the BC coast. Ten properties, a handful of creators, every pairing set up and verified by hand. Promo codes and a simple tracked link — no platform required.

NextAutomate the proof

Conversion pixel plus PMS webhooks (Guesty, Hostaway), instant commission splits, and creator self-serve across the whole network.

ThenScale through the pipes

PMS app marketplaces — one integration reaching thousands of properties — and publishing the conversion data the pilot produced.

AlwaysDensity before coverage

Twenty bookable places in one region beats two hundred scattered. Each region gets proven before the next one opens.

The question the pilot answers: does creator content convert to direct bookings at a rate that pays everyone? If the honest answer is no, that's worth knowing early.
