Pricing

Free to join. One number after that.

Ten percent of a booking a creator's content demonstrably drove, charged only when the stay actually completes. Split evenly: 5% to the creator in cash, 5% to FETCH. There is no second fee anywhere on this page.

How a $1,500 booking breaks down

$1,500Traveller books a stay
$450What an OTA could take
$150What FETCH costs (10%)

$1,350Stays with the property

The traveller pays the same either way — same room, same rate, same booking page. The only thing that changes is who gets credit for the demand and how much of the money stays in the region.

What each side pays

Creators

Nothing. You get paid.

  • 5% of booking value, in cash, on every verified completed stay
  • No fee to join, list, tag content or request a hosted stay
  • No revenue share on your other sponsorships or affiliate work
  • No minimum audience, no minimum posting, no exclusivity

Properties & DMCs

10%, on completed stays only.

  • Charged only on bookings carrying a creator's code or tracked link
  • Nothing owed on cancellations, no-shows or bookings you'd have had anyway
  • No listing fee, subscription, setup charge or minimum spend
  • No charge to host a creator or to invite one

Things FETCH will never charge for

Every one of these is a fee somebody in this industry currently charges. Adding any of them would make FETCH another toll, which is the thing it was built to remove.

Listing or joining

Free for properties, DMCs and creators, permanently. Getting into the network costs nothing.

Subscriptions

No monthly platform fee, no tiers, no "pro" plan that unlocks the useful part.

Hosted stays

No FAM fee, no matchmaking fee, no placement fee. Arranging a hosted stay is a feature, not a product.

Clicks, views or tags

Nothing is charged per tap, per impression or per tagged post. Only a completed booking generates anything.

Featured placement

You can't buy your way up the network. Ranking comes from verified conversions, or it means nothing.

Payouts

Creators aren't charged to withdraw what they earned. The 5% is the 5%.

The take-rate promise

Ten percent, split evenly, is the whole business model. Not an introductory rate, not a pilot discount, not a number that creeps up once there's enough density to make leaving painful. The day FETCH charges like an OTA, it has become the problem it was built to replace — so it's written into how the company is run, not into a promotions page.

Is 10% an introductory rate?

No. It's the rate. There's no pilot discount that expires and no plan to raise it once the network is dense.

Can a property offer more than 5% to a creator?

Yes — a property can boost the creator's share for a season or a campaign to compete for attention. FETCH's half never rises above 5%.

When is commission actually due?

On a completed stay, confirmed by the property at monthly reconciliation. A booking that cancels never generates an invoice. See the verification loop.

Who pays the 10% — the property or the traveller?

The property, out of a booking it would otherwise have paid an OTA more for. The traveller's price is untouched.

What about taxes and payouts across borders?

Creators are paid as independent contractors and are responsible for their own tax. Pilot payouts run monthly; automated instant splits come with the full platform.

Nothing to pay until something works.

Joining costs nothing on either side. The first money that moves is a commission on a stay that already happened.
