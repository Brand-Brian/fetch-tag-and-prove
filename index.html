Tag. Book. Prove.

1

Tag

A creator tags a place in content they already make. FETCH generates a tracked link and a promo code for that exact pairing.

2

Book

A traveler taps through and books direct on the property's own booking page. Same price they'd pay anywhere. FETCH never touches the payment.

3

Prove

The booking is verified, and a 10% commission splits instantly: 5% to the creator in cash, 5% to FETCH. Both sides see the proof.
