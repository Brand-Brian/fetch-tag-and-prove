How a month actually runs

Through the month

Creator links send taps to your booking page. Codes land on reservations. Everything logs itself.

At month end

FETCH sends one statement listing every claimed booking against its code or link.

You confirm

You tick off which stays actually completed. Anything you don't confirm isn't billed.

One invoice

A single 10% commission on confirmed stays. The creator's half is paid out from it.

Property questions

How do I know the booking really came from a creator?

Two independent layers: the tracked link that landed on your booking page, and the unique code on the reservation. You see the same record we do, and you confirm the stay before anything is owed. See the attribution model.

Will this cannibalise bookings I'd have got anyway?

Commission attaches only to bookings carrying a creator's link or code. A guest who finds you by search, referral or repeat stay costs you nothing — and if you think a claim is wrong, you decline it at reconciliation.

Do I have to discount for the creator's audience?

No. There's no traveller discount in the model. The code is an attribution marker, not a promotion — though you're free to attach an offer to it if you want one.

What if I use Guesty, Hostaway or a channel manager?

Great — automated verification plugs into those later so reconciliation disappears. During the pilot it's a monthly confirmation, which takes minutes.

Can I work with only a few creators?

Yes. Some properties want one long-term partner; others want a rotating handful. You approve every pairing, and you can end one at any time.

Is FETCH going to become an OTA?

No — and we've written it down. FETCH never holds traveller payment, never becomes merchant of record, never builds a competing booking engine, and never raises the take rate. The four rules.
