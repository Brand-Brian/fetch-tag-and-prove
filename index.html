How the money moves

No invoices to chase, no gray areas. The commission follows the stay, not the click.

1 · Booked

A traveler books direct using a creator's link or code. The pairing is logged the moment it happens.

2 · Stayed

The guest checks out. Cancellations and no-shows never trigger a commission — nobody gets paid on a trip that didn't happen.

3 · Verified

The completed stay is confirmed with the property against the code or tracked link. Both sides see the same record.

4 · Paid

The 10% splits: 5% to the creator in cash, 5% to FETCH. The property pays one simple commission — a third of the OTA toll.

How we prove it

Attribution is the whole product, so it runs on two layers at once.

The tracked link

Every creator-property pairing gets its own link. It lives in bios, Stories stickers, and auto-DMs — and it carries the creator's credit straight through to the property's own booking page.

The code

The same pairing gets a unique code. It survives everything links can't: screenshots, device switches, and the traveler who books on a laptop nine days after seeing the Reel. If the code shows up on a booking, the creator gets credit. Full stop.

Travelers never pay more for booking through a creator. Same room, same rate, same booking page — the only difference is who gets credit for the demand.

What FETCH will never do

Never hold the traveler's money.

Every booking completes on the property's own booking page. The property is always the merchant of record.

Never become another marketplace.

We don't want travelers searching FETCH. We want them booking you, direct, from the content that inspired them.

Never charge to join.

No listing fees, no subscriptions, no charges for hosted stays. If a booking doesn't happen, nobody pays anything.

Never creep the take rate.

10%, split evenly. The day we charge like an OTA, we've become the problem we were built to replace.

Your questions, answered

For creators

How big does my audience need to be?

There's no minimum. FETCH pays on conversion, not reach — a creator with 4,000 engaged followers who trust their taste can out-earn one with 400,000. The pilot is picked on fit, not follower count.

What content do I have to make?

Whatever you already make. FETCH works on existing content — tag a place you've already posted about and it becomes bookable. No scripts, no briefs, no approval queues. Your content stays yours.

When and how do I get paid?

After the guest's stay completes and the booking is verified, your 5% pays out in cash. Not credits, not discounts, not exposure. During the pilot, payouts run monthly; automated instant payouts come with the full platform.

What if someone sees my Reel but books a week later on their laptop?

That's exactly what the code is for. Links can break across devices; the code doesn't. If your code is on the booking, you get credit — no matter when or where it happens.

What about hosted stays?

Properties in the network can invite you for hosted stays — and you can request them. Hosted stays are always free to arrange, and any bookings your content drives afterward pay your full commission.

For properties & DMCs

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. No listing fee, no subscription, no setup charge. You pay one thing, ever: 10% on a completed stay that a creator's content demonstrably drove. A booking that doesn't happen costs you zero.

How do I know a booking really came from a creator?

Two ways at once: the tracked link that lands on your booking page, and the unique code on the reservation. You see the same proof record we do — nobody takes anyone's word for anything.

Do I need special technology?

If you can take a direct booking and honour a promo or reference code, you can join today. During the pilot you simply report codes you receive. Automated verification connects to your booking engine or PMS later — no rip-and-replace, ever.

What happens on a cancellation?

Nothing. Commission is only due on completed stays. Cancellations and no-shows never generate a charge.

Who owns the guest?

You do. The booking happens on your engine, the payment goes to you, and the guest data stays with you. FETCH proves where the demand came from — it never gets between you and your guest.

How do I choose which creators to work with?

Inside the network you can browse creators by audience, region, and — most importantly — verified booking conversions. You pick partners on proof of bookings, not follower counts, and invite the ones headed your way.
